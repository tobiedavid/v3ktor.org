Food Trial Log

Find the food that upsets your digestive system. About a week.

You eat a few plain foods until your gut calms. Then you add foods back one at a time. Whatever brings the loose stools back is your answer.

Open the app

Free. No account, no ads, no server. It never leaves your phone.

How it works

  1. Keep it boring. Eat only the plain foods on your starting list. Report every stool. After 2 good days in a row, you move on.
  2. Add one food a day. Easy foods first. If a food upsets you, it comes off your list and you go back to step 1.
  3. Show the result. The app keeps a list of the foods that upset you, and a summary you can show a doctor or a pharmacist.

If your loose stools come and go, there is a slower route: it cuts one food group at a time.

Put it on your phone

  • Android (Chrome): menu, then Install app.
  • iPhone (Safari): Share, then Add to Home Screen.

It works offline once it has loaded. Your log lives in this browser on this phone, so back it up from the app's menu.

Read this first

Blood in your stool, black stool, fainting, chest pain or trouble breathing: get care now. The app has a Something’s wrong button on every screen.

Food Trial Log is a log with rules, not medical or dietitian advice. A doctor or a dietitian hasn’t checked it yet. If you are pregnant, breastfeeding, or living with Crohn’s, colitis, celiac disease or an eating disorder, talk to your doctor before cutting foods. If you have had loose stools for weeks, ask your doctor for a celiac blood test before you start.
